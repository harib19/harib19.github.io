Aegis

Open-source RTL timing closure that won't touch a synchronizer it can't prove safe

An LLM suggests timing fixes, and Aegis doesn't trust any of them. Yosys and OpenSTA give the baseline, Yosys EQY checks every candidate for sequential equivalence, and a structural pass runs first. It finds synchronizer chains, Gray-coded pointers, reset crossings and clock dividers and locks them before the model sees anything. If a candidate edits a locked region, its checksum fails and it gets thrown out.
