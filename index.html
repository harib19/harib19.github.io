Aegis

Automated RTL timing closure with CDC guardrails

An LLM proposes timing rewrites; nothing it writes is trusted. Yosys and OpenSTA set the baseline, Yosys EQY gates every candidate on sequential equivalence, and a structural pre-pass classifies synchronizer chains, Gray-coded pointers, reset crossings and clock dividers into immutable tiers before generation begins. Candidates that touch a locked region are rejected on a checksum rather than scored.

The premise is that equivalence checking is necessary but not sufficient across clock domains. Multi-clock safety has to be enforced on structure, upstream of the model, because no downstream check can see metastability.
